## Original art (generated by script, no third-party content)

- `atlas.png` / `atlas.json`: town, campus, interior and stage art drawn by `tools/gen-town-art.py` (helpers in `tools/pixart.py`) and packed by `tools/build-atlas.py`.
- The HCMUT gate (blue glass pillar with the BK logo block, grey walls) and the central building (roof band, blue columns, canopy with planters, flag) are stylised pixel redraws made from reference photos of the campus supplied by the host; they are drawn by hand in `tools/hcmut_art.py` and no photo pixels are used. The BK / school name is the host's own university.
- `sprites/host-*.png`: the host in three outfits (freshman, student, graduate), `tools/gen-host-sprite.py`.
- `sprites/npc-*.png`: passers-by, palette swaps of the student sprite (`tools/gen-town-art.py`).
- `app/icon.svg`: tiny graduation-cap favicon.
